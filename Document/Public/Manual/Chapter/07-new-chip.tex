\chapter{Adding a New Chip to the RVM}\label{ch:7-adding-a-new-chip}
\section{Preparations for Adding a New Chip}\label{sec:7-1}

Adding a new chip requires completing three parts of work: the RME header file, the RVM header file, and the chip description file. The original draft only gives the headings for these three aspects and does not provide specific steps; the content of this section is to be supplemented in a later version.
